A clearer next step

FollowThrough supports follow-up coordination for community health workers.

  1. Find what needs attention. Select a summary card, use the queue filter, or search for a participant, barrier, or next action.
  2. Document the next step. Open a record, update its follow-up date, referral status, access barriers, and action.
  3. Record what happened. Save a contact or outcome note. Every saved change appears in the activity history.
  4. Close the loop. Complete a follow-up after its referral and recorded barriers are resolved, with an outcome note.
About this preview

All records are fictional. Data stays in this browser; it is not shared with a clinic or synchronised across devices. Export sample data to keep a copy.

This is an early software preview, not a clinical service. Do not enter real patient information.
